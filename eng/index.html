    About Us

    Executive Overview & Mandate

    Dalit Samaj Sewa Sangh (Dalit Society Service Organization – DSSO) is non-government organization led by disadvantaged community particularly established to insure Dalit rights, poverty reduction, human rights, leadership development, entrepreneur development, advocacy, youth empowerment, land rights, development as well as social justice and dignity. It was established in district administration of his Siraha in 2000 AD and also affiliated in Social Welfare Council in 2001 AD. It has been organizing, empowering and mobilizing Dalits, women, forced labour and adolescences against social discriminatory malpractices and evils in the community and country. Since the time of its establishment, DSSO has been working on the issue of Dalit rights, human trafficking, modern slavery and forced labour. It has strong coordination, collaboration and networking as well as alliance with government line agencies, elected representatives, civil society organizations, issue based movements, trade unions, sister organizations of various political parties, duty bearers and concerned stakeholders.

    Vision, Mission and Goal (VMG) Strategic Framework:

    Vision / दूरदृष्टि

    Creation of a just, equitable society free from discrimination, inequality, and exploitation across state and community.

    Mission / ध्येय

    Organize and empower Madhesi Dalits through leadership development to advocate for fundamental human rights and public services.

    Goal / लक्ष्य

    Promote socioeconomic upliftment of Madhesi Dalits through awareness campaigns and assertive representation in local governance.

    Core Institutional Objectives & Rights Priorities:

    • 01 Protect and promote fundamental human rights, dignity, and constitutional justice.
    • 02 Advocate for affirmative rights for Madhesi Dalits, landless Harawa-Charawa, and youth.
    • 03 Enhance accessibility to government social protection schemes and public resources.
    • 04 Promote sustainable income-generation and livelihood diversification opportunities.
    • 05 Support quality education access and school retention for children from marginalized Dalit families.
    • 06 Spearhead campaigns against caste untouchability, child marriage, dowry, and bonded labor.
